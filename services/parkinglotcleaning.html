Parking Lot Cleaning

Sweeping and Pressure Cleaning for Active Lots

Parking lots are one of the first areas tenants, customers and visitors see. Our combined approach can include power sweeping to clear debris and dirt, pressure cleaning for gum, grime and stains, and hot-water treatment for oil spots.

We service lots across Miami-Dade and Broward on recurring monthly, biweekly or weekly programs, scheduled at night or off-hours around your traffic. One-time deep cleans are also available.

  • Retail, office, industrial and HOA lots
  • Sweeping + pressure cleaning combined
  • Oil-spot and gum treatment
  • Night and off-hours scheduling

Our Approach

Sweeping and Pressure Cleaning, Combined

  • Power sweeping to clear loose debris before pressure cleaning
  • Hot-water treatment for oil and grease stains
  • Self-contained water tanks where hookups are limited
  • Backpack blowers for curbs, corners and under parked cars
  • Active dust control on every sweeper
  • Operator leaves a service card after each visit
  • Scheduling around traffic and local noise ordinances
