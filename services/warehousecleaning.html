Warehouse Cleaning

Cleaning Plans for Industrial Floors and Surfaces

Our warehouse work centers on concrete floors and other cleanable industrial surfaces. Depending on the facility, the plan may combine power scrubbing for broad floor areas with pressure cleaning for walls, columns and areas the scrubber cannot reach.

We assess the surface, buildup, access and operating schedule before recommending a one-time deep clean or recurring program. The exact method and drying time depend on conditions at the property.

  • Ride-on scrubbing with wash-water recovery
  • Degreaser selection based on the surface and buildup
  • Brushes selected for painted, sealed or unfinished floors
  • Pressure cleaning where access and surface conditions allow

Services We Offer

Industrial Floor and Surface Cleaning

We scope each job around the cleanable surfaces, equipment access and operating schedule at the facility.

01

Floor Scrubbing

Ride-on scrubbers with a 5-foot deck remove dust, oil and stains while recovering much of the wash water.

02

Degreasing

Degreasers are selected according to the surface and the type of oil, carbon or grease buildup present.

03

Girder & Overhead Cleaning

Pressure cleaning may be used for accessible girders, walls and overhead surfaces when conditions allow.

04

Surface-Matched Brushes

Brush or pad type is selected for the floor finish and buildup; softer options help reduce abrasion on painted or sealed surfaces.

05

Pressure Cleaning

Walls, columns and smaller areas can be included alongside floor cleaning in a coordinated scope.

06

Flexible Programs

Single, weekly, monthly or annual service scheduled around the facility's operating hours.

How We Work

Equipment Matched to the Facility

  • Power scrubbing planned around equipment access and operations
  • 5-foot scrub deck for fast, even coverage
  • Scrub & vacuum in a single pass
  • Wash-water recovery helps reduce drying time; conditions vary
  • Degreasers selected for oil, carbon or grease buildup
  • Softer brushes for painted or sealed floors
  • Pressure cleaning for hard-to-reach areas
  • Free on-site assessment of your facility
