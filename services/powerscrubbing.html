Power Scrubbing

Cleaning Large Concrete Floors

Power scrubbing is a practical option for cleaning large concrete areas. Our ride-on scrubbers use rotating brushes on a 5-foot deck, with cleaning products selected for the surface and buildup.

The equipment scrubs and recovers water in a single pass, which helps reduce drying time and interruption to the property.

  • Designed for large concrete areas
  • Sweeping needs evaluated by property conditions
  • Ride-on, single-pass operation
  • Water recovery helps reduce drying time

Why Power Scrubbing

A Practical Option for Large Concrete Areas

Ideal for garages, warehouses, retail floors and open concrete surfaces across South Florida.

01

Fast Coverage

A 5-foot scrub deck covers large areas quickly, so even big floors are done in a single visit.

02

Scrubbing With Water Recovery

The machine scrubs and recovers water during the same pass, reducing the number of separate cleaning steps.

03

Reduced Drying Time

Water recovery helps prepare the floor to return to service after cleaning.
