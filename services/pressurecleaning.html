Pressure Cleaning

Commercial Cleaning for High-Traffic Surfaces

Dirt, mildew, oil and tire marks build up fast on high-traffic concrete. Commercial-grade pressure cleaning can address that buildup and help maintain the appearance of the surface over time.

Watch on YouTube ↗

For parking garages, storefronts, sidewalks and South Florida shopping centers, we match the water temperature, pressure and attachments to the property and the material being cleaned.

  • Hot- & cold-water washing
  • Self-contained water tanks — no hookup needed
  • Surface cleaners for consistent coverage
  • Non-marking equipment selected for the surface

What We Clean

Surfaces We Clean

We assess the material, buildup and access conditions before selecting the cleaning method.

01

Garages & Lots

Cleaning for oil, tire marks and grime on parking decks, ramps and stalls — including sites without a nearby water hookup.

02

Sidewalks & Entrances

Cleaning for mildew, gum and dirt on commercial entrances and walkways.

03

Building Exteriors

Facades, walls, columns and overhangs cleaned with pressure selected for the surface.

04

Dumpster & Service Areas

Hot-water cleaning and degreasing for back-of-house areas where grime and odor can build up.

05

Road Dividers & Tunnels

Targeted pressure cleaning and striping preparation for roadways, dividers, walls and tunnel interiors.

06

Loading Docks & Ramps

Cleaning for heavy-traffic surfaces that supports a cleaner, more presentable operation.

The Right Equipment

Equipment for Large Commercial Jobs

  • 8 gallons-per-minute high-output pumps
  • Large onboard water tanks for hookup-free sites
  • Surface cleaners ranging from 18" to 36"
  • Turbo nozzles for tight, even cleaning
  • Hot-water treatments for set-in oil & grease
  • InfraWash-developed hydraulic boom attachments for walls and hard-to-reach surfaces
  • Striping preparation & road-divider cleaning
  • Non-marking hose to protect finished surfaces
