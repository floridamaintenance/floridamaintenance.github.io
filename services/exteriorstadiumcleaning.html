Exterior Stadium Cleaning

Keep High-Traffic Venues Ready for Guests

Stadiums, arenas and event venues pick up gum, spills, tire marks, dirt, mildew and back-of-house buildup fast. We clean the exterior concrete, walkways, service areas and traffic zones that shape the guest experience before anyone reaches a seat.

Our Miami-based crew works around event calendars, vendor access, load-ins and active properties. The scope can combine pressure cleaning, power sweeping and soft washing according to the surface and access conditions.

  • Entrances, concourses and exterior walkways
  • Service tunnels, loading docks and back-of-house areas
  • Parking approaches, drop-off zones and traffic lanes
  • One-time event cleanup or recurring maintenance
  • Soft washing for signage, stucco and painted surfaces

What We Clean

Exterior Venue Cleaning

We focus on the high-traffic exterior areas guests, staff, vendors and vehicles use before, during and after events.

01

Entrances & Walkways

Pressure cleaning for front gates, plazas, exterior walkways, curb lines and arrival areas.

02

Exterior Walls & Columns

Pressure cleaning or soft washing for concrete, masonry, columns, signage and painted surfaces.

03

Service Tunnels

Surface cleaning and sweeping for tunnel approaches, service roads and covered access areas.

04

Loading Docks

Degreasing, debris removal and pressure washing for vendor zones and back-of-house work areas.

05

Parking & Drop-Off Areas

Sweeping and pressure cleaning for traffic lanes, staging areas, ramps and guest drop-off zones.

06

Event Turnaround Cleaning

One-time cleanups before major events or scheduled maintenance between event dates.
