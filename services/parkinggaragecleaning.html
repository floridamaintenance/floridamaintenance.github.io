Parking Garage Cleaning

Cleaner Garages, Planned Around Active Traffic

Parking garages collect oil, tire marks, dust, trash and standing grime fast. We combine pressure cleaning, power scrubbing and power sweeping for concrete decks, ramps, curbs, columns and pedestrian areas.

Every program depends on the property. A common approach is monthly sweeping for lighter routine maintenance and quarterly pressure cleaning for a deeper clean, with the frequency and service mix adjusted to the garage.

Our crews plan work around active South Florida properties using low-clearance equipment, self-contained water capacity and scheduling options for nights, weekends or recurring maintenance.

  • Decks, ramps, stalls and drive lanes
  • Oil stain and tire-mark treatment
  • Low-clearance garage equipment
  • One-time or recurring service plans

What We Clean

Complete Garage Maintenance

From daily traffic lanes to hard-to-reach corners, we bring the right mix of equipment for every part of the garage.

01

Parking Decks

Concrete decks, parking stalls and drive lanes cleaned of dirt, tire marks, spills and daily buildup.

02

Ramps & Curbs

High-traffic ramps, turns, curbs and wheel stops cleaned with pressure washing and scrubbing equipment.

03

Oil & Grease Stains

Targeted degreasing and hot-water cleaning for set-in vehicle leaks and high-stain areas.

04

Stairwells & Entrances

Pedestrian paths, elevator lobbies and entry areas cleaned to support a well-maintained first impression.

05

Curbs & Hard-to-Reach Corners

Backpack blowers clear debris from behind curbs, wheel stops and corners, then move it into the sweeper truck's path to be vacuumed up.

06

Recurring Programs

Weekly, monthly, twice-yearly or custom schedules based on the garage's traffic and maintenance needs.

Our Approach

Built Around Active Garages

  • Pressure cleaning for concrete, curbs and columns
  • Power scrubbing for large open deck areas
  • Backpack blowers for corners, curbs and wheel stops
  • Power sweeping to vacuum up staged debris and loose dirt
  • Hot-water treatment for oil and grease stains
  • Self-contained water tanks where hookups are limited
  • Low-clearance equipment for parking structures
  • Phased scheduling designed to reduce traffic impacts
  • One-time deep cleans or recurring maintenance
