What We Do

Our Core Services

Four proven cleaning methods — pressure cleaning, power sweeping, power scrubbing and soft washing — matched to the surface and the job.

Pressure Cleaning

Commercial-grade hot- and cold-water pressure washing for concrete, sidewalks, service areas, facades and high-traffic exterior surfaces.

Learn more

Power Sweeping

Truck-mounted sweeping that clears debris and surface grime from lots, garages, roadways and tunnels with active dust control.

Power Scrubbing

Ride-on scrubbers that clean large concrete floors in a single pass and leave them dry and traffic-ready within minutes.

Soft Washing

Our Story

It Started with a Practical Challenge.

Florida Maintenance began in 2014 with a specialized cleaning project at the Miami Tunnel. Its architectural panels needed a touchless cleaning approach, so a young company owner taught himself the skills needed to build the first prototype.

The equipment improved over the years. Continued development and fabrication are now handled through InfraWash, an affiliated company under common ownership, while Florida Maintenance operates the purpose-built equipment across four core services for commercial and public properties throughout South Florida.
