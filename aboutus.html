Serving South Florida since 2014

The right equipment for the work.

Florida Maintenance began at the PortMiami Tunnel in 2014. Today, we work with facility managers, property managers and government agencies across four core services and a range of commercial and public properties.

Our beginning

The PortMiami Tunnel was our first project.

The tunnel's architectural panels needed a touchless cleaning approach rather than a conventional mechanical scrubber.

Florida Maintenance was established in 2014 to take on that work. Its young company owner taught himself the skills needed to build the first prototype.

The original contract was later extended, and the founder continued refining the trucks over the years. The first spray tower gradually developed into a system with a hydraulic boom arm and attachments for tunnel panels, highway barrier walls, sound walls and other infrastructure surfaces. Continued equipment development and fabrication are now handled through InfraWash, an affiliated company under common ownership.

We've cleaned the PortMiami Tunnel since it opened. Our work now also includes highway corridors, arena and stadium exteriors, parking garages and other commercial properties across South Florida.
